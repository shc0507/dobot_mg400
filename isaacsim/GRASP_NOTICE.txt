Isaac Sim physical gripper overlay (2026-10-13)

Assets: mg400_grasp.usda and grasp_scene.usda.
Rebuild: scripts/build_isaac_grasp.py.
These assets reference the original mg400_with_gripper.usd and scene.usda;
the base model, its source attribution and licenses remain unchanged. See
NOTICE.txt and LICENSE. DH-Robotics meshes retain their separate provenance;
the HarvestX Apache-2.0 license does not license the DH-Robotics geometry.

Physical changes:
  - Two original jaw-carrier meshes and estimated fingertips are deactivated
    in the base reference and reproduced under two movable rigid jaw links.
  - A fixed housing mass is added at the existing PGEA flange mounting frame.
  - Two opposed prismatic joints travel inward 0 to 0.005 m per jaw.
    Zero preserves the source CAD pose. The estimated fingertip inner gap is
    0.0119 m open and 0.0019 m closed. Reference frames remain unchanged.
  - Only the fingertip boxes receive gripper collision geometry. Internal
    racks, cable, housing and arm CAD meshes remain non-colliding visuals.
  - The physical-gripper scene adds a ground collider. Its runtime grasp
    example adds a support and a free 0.005 kg, 8 x 8 x 20 mm test object.

Manufacturer specifications used for PGEA-2-10:
  Total stroke 10 mm, total mass 0.15 kg, force 0.8 to 2 N per jaw.
  Source: https://en.dh-robotics.com/product/pgea
  Catalog: https://en.dh-robotics.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/DH_PGEAPGIA-catalog_V255.pdf
  The existing PGEA-2/15-10 O-S CAD is shared between force variants; this
  simulation's 2 N limit does not establish the actual hardware variant.

Simulation assumptions, not measured hardware parameters:
  Housing mass 0.13 kg, each moving jaw plus tip 0.01 kg; box inertias.
  The existing 10 x 9 x 15 mm fingertips are estimated from a photograph,
  not manufacturer CAD. Their net gap differs from the stock carrier gap.
  Linear force drives: maximum 2 N, stiffness 1000 N/m, damping 10 N s/m.
  Static/dynamic friction 1.0, restitution 0, contact offset 0.0002 m,
  rest offset 0. Articulation solver iterations: position 32, velocity 8.
  Housing and jaws have gravity enabled; the original arm links retain
  disabled gravity and no self-collision geometry.

The runtime test uses drives after its single initial robot-state assignment.
The test object remains free and gravity-enabled, without attachment joints
or subsequent scripted pose assignment. Arm coupling remains coordinated
drive commands to eight joints, not physical closed-loop linkage constraints.
Passing this test demonstrates the selected simulated contact/drive setup;
it does not validate hardware gripping performance or whole-arm dynamics.
