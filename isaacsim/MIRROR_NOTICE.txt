MG400 and PGEA measured-position display

Assets: mg400_mirror.usda and workspace_scene.usda.
Builders: scripts/build_isaac_mirror.py and scripts/build_isaac_workspace.py.
These assets reference mg400_with_gripper.usd and scene.usda.
The base model, source attribution and licenses remain unchanged.
See NOTICE.txt and LICENSE. DH-Robotics meshes retain their separate provenance.
The HarvestX Apache-2.0 license does not license the DH-Robotics geometry.

Display structure:
  - Two original jaw-carrier meshes and estimated fingertips move under
    separate jaw transforms. Their original instances are inactive.
  - A housing transform follows the existing PGEA flange mounting frame.
  - Two opposed prismatic joints define 0 to 0.005 m inward travel per jaw.
    Zero travel preserves the source CAD pose and reference frames.
  - The mirror code uses the fixed and prismatic joint frames to display
    measured positions. The new housing and jaws have no rigid-body masses,
    force drives, collision geometry or contact materials.
  - The workspace contains the robot, ground, lights and camera.
    Its physics scene is inactive. Keep the physics timeline stopped.

Geometry limits:
  - The manufacturer specifies 10 mm total stroke for PGEA-2-10.
    Source: https://en.dh-robotics.com/product/pgea
  - The existing 10 x 9 x 15 mm fingertips are estimates from a photograph.
    They are not manufacturer CAD. Their inner gap is 0.0119 m when open
    and 0.0019 m at full inward travel.
  - The base model retains its arm joint and rigid-body metadata.
    The mirror uses the controller-to-eight-joint mapping to display the arm.
    These assets do not validate gripping performance or robot dynamics.
