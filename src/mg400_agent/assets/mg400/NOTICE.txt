Modifications made for this project:
  - Expanded the URDF and translated its body hierarchy, joint origins, axes,
    limits, visual transforms, masses, centers of mass and full inertias to MJCF.
  - Converted Collada triangle geometry to binary STL after applying each
    bound visual-scene transform. The source declares metres and Z up;
    additional node scales/rotations are baked into the STL vertex positions.
  - Kept diffuse material groups separate, applying their source colors in MJCF.
    Material reflectivity and textures are not reproduced. Zero-area triangles
    are omitted. Original Collada contributor metadata identifies Blender User
    and Blender as the author/authoring tool for these exported meshes.
  - Added a fixed ground, lighting, an overview camera, and named frame sites.
  - scene.xml disables all geom contacts and gravity. It has no actuators or
    linkage constraints and is for qpos + mj_forward kinematic playback.
  - dynamics.xml preserves the same CAD body hierarchy, origins and inertias,
    adds gravity, four position servos and four ideal scalar linkage constraints.
    Two joint equalities couple j2_2=j2_1 and j3_2=-j2_1. Fixed tendon sums couple
    j4_1=-(j2_1+j3_1) and j4_2=j2_1+j3_1; another fixed tendon transmits the third
    controller axis q3=j2_1+j3_1. These reproduce the controller's angle relations;
    they do not model physical cable routing, gearbox compliance or pin contacts.
  - dynamics.xml uses a 0.002 s timestep and implicitfast integrator. Position
    gains (200,200,200,20), velocity gains (8,4,4,0.5), actuator torque caps
    (20,20,20,3) N m, joint damping 0.05 and joint armature 0.001 are simulation
    tuning values, not measured hardware parameters. Equality solref=(0.004,1)
    and solimp=(0.999,0.999,0.001) are numerical solver settings.
  - All geom contacts remain disabled in both models. The gripper is visual only.
    Neither asset is a validated dynamics, collision, grasping or safety model.

Coordinates and control mapping:
  All positions are metres and joint angles radians. The fixed base is at the
  world origin. mg400_controller_origin is (-0.005, 0, 0.224) from mg400_base;
  its axes are parallel. mg400_flange is the exact URDF flange frame, and
  mg400_tcp coincides with it because no gripper/tool offset has been configured.
  Four controller axes (q1, q2, q3, q4) map to the eight named joints as follows:
    mg400_j1=q1, mg400_j2_1=q2, mg400_j2_2=q2, mg400_j3_1=q3-q2,
    mg400_j3_2=-q2, mg400_j4_1=-q3, mg400_j4_2=q3, mg400_j5=q4.
  Playback must assign joints by name; their MuJoCo storage order follows the
  branched body tree. scene.xml retains the URDF's broad joint ranges, which
  must not be interpreted as the controller's safe operating limits.
  dynamics.xml uses reference ranges from the pinned kinematics.hpp: q1 and q4
  [-160,160], q2 [-25,85], q3 [-25,105], q3-q2 [-60,60] degrees, with dependent
  joint ranges derived from the same mapping. The conditional q2 minimum of
  -19 degrees when q3 <= 0 and the source's 1.05 degree planning margin are not
  represented by the static MJCF limits; apply them when validating commands.
  These source ranges are uncalibrated references, not a hardware safety claim.
  The four actuator names are mg400_q1_position through mg400_q4_position;
  their control setpoints are controller angles in radians. Initialize a valid
  coupled pose once, then drive ctrl and mj_step without rewriting qpos.

Known analytical FK approximation:
  mg400_common/include/mg400_common/kinematics.hpp in the pinned source uses
  rounded LINK1=(.0435,0,0), LINK2=(0,0,.175), LINK3=(.175,0,0), and
  LINK4=(.066,0,-.053). The URDF retains CAD offsets and a shoulder height
  .004 m above the controller origin. At all-zero controller angles:
    Exact URDF flange, base frame: (0.2784487429828484,
      -0.0000001243781102, 0.353251164344716) m.
    Exact URDF flange, controller frame: (0.2834487429828484,
      -0.0000001243781102, 0.129251164344716) m.
    Rounded analytical FK, controller frame: (0.2845, 0, 0.122) m.
  This approximately 7.327 mm disagreement is not numerical solver error.
  Its positional offset varies with the pose; no constant correction has been
  applied. Validate this exact CAD chain separately from the rounded FK, and
  calibrate the real robot/tool before using simulated poses for hardware.
