A little movement. A lot of control.

Choose a speed, then hold a direction to move until release, or set your step count and click a direction button for a fixed move. Use Up/Down for bottom, Right/Left for top, and Insert In/Insert Out for insert. Each channel moves independently.

Set speedMoveWait 1s
Counts are signed STEP pulses. Reset and homing reset the reference. Travel per pulse depends on the mode. There is no encoder feedback. Stopped motors hold position for 10 s by default, then their drivers sleep and stop holding.
